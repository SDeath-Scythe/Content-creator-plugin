# Content Pillars — test.pricing.design
<!-- version: 1.0 · updated: 2026-10-24 · derived from: brand-profile v1.0 -->

## The set at a glance
- ⭐ **Price it from your numbers** — build a quote from hours, costs and value, not guesses — ~35% of mix
- **Say it to the client** — word-for-word lines for price pushback, discounts and revisions — ~30%
- **Gulf freelance money habits** — deposits, invoices, currency, scope, payment terms — ~20%
- **The PRICE calculator** — how to use the free sheet (the one conversion pillar) — ~15%

---

## ⭐ Pillar 1 — Price it from your numbers
- **Covers:** hourly rate vs project rate, costs, taxes/overheads, how many billable hours you really have.
- **Why we own it:** it is the core of the free calculator and the page's one point of view (pricing is a method).
- **For:** junior freelance designers who guess their price.
- **Goal it serves:** trust, then leads.
- **Example angles:** 1) your hourly rate is wrong because billable hours are fewer than you think; 2) what to add for software and revisions; 3) project price vs hourly for a logo; 4) the cost of a "small" extra request; 5) why copying a price you saw is risky; 6) price for rush work; 7) when a low price costs more; 8) rounding a quote the right way.
- **Draws on:** the calculator's logic; illustrative examples labelled "مثال".

## Pillar 2 — Say it to the client
- **Covers:** scripts and replies for "it's expensive", "can you discount", "one more small edit".
- **Why we own it:** the page gives lines to say in Gulf dialect, not theory.
- **For:** same segment, at the moment of fear.
- **Goal it serves:** shares and saves (sends to a friend).
- **Example angles:** 1) reply to "غالي"; 2) reply to "خصم؟"; 3) how to cap revisions; 4) ask for deposit; 5) say no to free work "for exposure"; 6) raising your price for an old client; 7) quoting without apologising.
- **Draws on:** voice, dialect, owner's lived examples (to collect).

## Pillar 3 — Gulf freelance money habits
- **Covers:** deposits, invoices, payment terms, currency (SAR/AED), contracts basics at a non-legal level.
- **Why we own it:** Gulf-specific context generic English accounts skip.
- **For:** same segment.
- **Goal it serves:** authority.
- **Example angles:** 1) 50% deposit rule; 2) invoice details to include; 3) late payment message; 4) one-page scope sheet; 5) pricing for local vs foreign clients; 6) saving for slow months; 7) tracking time.
- **Draws on:** owner's knowledge; no legal claims, flag for review.

## Pillar 4 — The PRICE calculator
- **Covers:** how to use each part of the free sheet, with examples.
- **Why we own it:** it is the lead magnet.
- **For:** viewers close to commenting.
- **Goal it serves:** leads.
- **Example angles:** 1) 60-second walk-through; 2) fill it for a logo job; 3) the field everyone skips; 4) what to do with the result.
- **Draws on:** the Google Sheet.

## Mix & balance
- **Signature pillar:** Price it from your numbers.
- **Rough split:** 35 / 30 / 20 / 15.
- **Promotion:** only pillar 4 promotes; every reel still carries the PRICE CTA only where the deliverable fits.
- **Notes:** no income claims in any pillar.
