# Brand Profile — test.pricing.design
<!-- version: 1.0 · last updated: 2026-10-24 · review: quarterly · type: personal -->

## Identity
- **What it is (one sentence):** An Instagram page that teaches freelance graphic designers in the Gulf how to price their work, with a free pricing calculator.
- **Category:** Freelance business education for designers.
- **Founded / location:** Gulf region (Saudi time zone, Asia/Riyadh). Owner details not given.

## Positioning
- **What's different:** Pricing help made for Gulf designers, in Gulf dialect, with a ready calculator, not generic English freelancing advice.
- **Why believe it:** Not stated yet. Open question: owner's own experience. No proof invented.
- **Point of view:** (1) Undercharging is a pricing-method problem, not a talent problem. (2) A price is built from your costs, time and value, not copied from other designers. (3) Say your price calmly, without apologising.
- **Won't say:** Anything that promises income. "Get rich". Names of other designers. Shaming clients or other designers.

## Audience
### Segment 1 — Undercharging junior freelancers
- **Who:** Graphic designers 22-32, 1-3 years freelancing in the Gulf.
- **Cares about:** Getting paid fairly, not losing clients, knowing what to quote.
- **Believes / objects:** "If I raise my price they will leave." "I'm still new." (assumed, to validate)
- **Where:** Instagram, scrolling Reels, sound often on in Arabic content.
- **Sophistication:** Strong on design, beginner on pricing.
- **Transformation:** Guessing a number and discounting, to quoting from a formula with confidence.

## Voice
- **Dimensions:** Formality 2/5 · Warmth 4/5 · Authority 3/5 · Energy 3/5 · Humor 2/5 · Polish 3/5 (provisional)
- **We sound** like a slightly older designer friend who has made the mistakes, **never** like a guru or a lecturer.
- **Words we use:** سعر، عرض سعر، ساعتك، مشروع، عميل، تعديلات، دفعة مقدمة، تسعّر (Gulf register).
- **Words we ban:** "ثراء", "اصرف", "مضمون", "أرباح خيالية", guru-speak, hype.
- **How we handle hard things:** Calm and practical. Client pushback = a script to say, not an argument.
- **Signature devices:** Open with one blunt line, one concrete number example, close with the PRICE CTA or a question.
- **Voice by platform:** Instagram only.
- **Pronunciation guide:** none needed yet (owner records own voice).
