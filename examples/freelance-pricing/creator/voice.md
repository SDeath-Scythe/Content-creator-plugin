Built from the brand interview only, not from writing samples. Nothing below is quoted from the owner. The voice test was not run (no real sample to compare against).

## Voice fingerprint
1. Opens with one blunt line that names the mistake ("سعرك غلط، وأنت اللي تدفع الثمن").
2. Gulf dialect, short spoken sentences, 5 to 9 words each.
3. One concrete example number per reel, always labelled "مثال".
4. Speaks to "you" (أنت) directly, as a friend who made the same mistake.
5. Gives a line to say to the client, not just advice.
6. Closes with the PRICE CTA or a one-line question.

## Never
- Never promises income or results ("بتكسب كذا").
- Never says "get rich" or shows income screenshots.
- Never names another designer.
- Never mocks clients or other designers.
- Never uses stiff Modern Standard Arabic in spoken lines; no English filler in on-screen text unless it is a term (PDF, deposit).

## Lexicon
- **Register:** Gulf dialect, plain.
- **Contractions:** Natural dialect forms (وش، ليش، كذا، الحين، مرة، يبغى).
- **Signature words & phrases:** عرض سعر، ساعتك، التعديلات، الدفعة المقدمة، "قول له كذا".
- **Banned words:** ثراء، مضمون، سر، خيالي، أسهل طريقة.
- **Concreteness rule:** one number example per reel.

## Syntax & rhythm
- **Sentence pattern:** short punches; one longer sentence for the payoff.
- **Openers:** a statement or a question, never a greeting.
- **Fragments:** yes.
- **Voice:** active.
- **Punctuation rhythm:** line breaks over commas; no exclamation chains.

## Rhetoric
- **Direct address:** high.
- **Devices:** contrast (what you charge vs what it costs you), a script to say.
- **Humor:** light, self-aware, never at clients' expense.
- **The turn / landing:** the reframe, then the CTA.

## Structure
- **Opening move:** the mistake, said plainly.
- **Build:** problem, quick math, line to say.
- **Close:** "اكتب PRICE" or a question.
- **Formatting:** short lines; Arabic captions with English first line.

## Stance
- **Distance & posture:** peer who is a step ahead.
- **Confidence:** assertive on method, honest about examples being examples.
- **Emotional register:** warm, steady.
- **Self-disclosure:** none assumed; do not invent personal stories.
- **For / against:** for pricing from a formula; against guessing and apologising.

## Tone map
- **Reels:** fastest, shortest lines, hook in 3 seconds.
- **Carousels:** one idea per slide, a little more explanation.
- **Replies / community:** brief, warm, point to the DM.
- **Bad news / crisis:** calm, no defensiveness.
- **Celebration / wins:** only real ones from followers, with permission.

## Do / don't rewrites

| "Here are 5 tips to grow your income." | "خمس أخطاء تخليك تسعّر أقل من شغلك." |
