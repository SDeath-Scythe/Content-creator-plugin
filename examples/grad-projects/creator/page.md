# Page settings

Example page, made up to show content-creator's output. Written in the shape `page-setup` produces; the interview itself wasn't run.

## Goal
Leads. Final-year students comment a keyword and get a free template: the week-by-week project plan (PROJECT) or the chapter-by-chapter report template (REPORT). Later: paid 1:1 project reviews.

## Niche and audience
Helping university students build their graduation project, and secondly write the graduation report.
Final-year students (computer science, engineering, IT), one or two semesters from graduating, stuck between a big idea and a deadline.

## Language
English for this example. On screen and in captions.

## Platforms
Instagram first; TikTok and YouTube Shorts with their own captions.

## Formats and cadence
Faceless reels built in code, voiced by the page owner. 3 reels a week, 1 carousel. Build day: Sunday.

## Posting hour
Evenings, when students are working on their projects. Only post when you can reply for 30 to 60 minutes.

## Call to action
Comment PROJECT (week-by-week plan) or REPORT (report template). The DM sends the file. Rotate with "save this for your final year".

## Look
Background #0E1226, paper #FBF8F1, ink #1A1E36, highlighter #FFD43B, red pen #E5484D, green #22B07D.
Covers: the hook in white over the document the reel is about, with a stamp.

## Banned claims
No grade guarantees. No "we'll do your project for you". No writing reports for students: we teach the structure.

## Tools
Example run: built-in renderer (Node 22, ffmpeg, Playwright). No connectors used.
