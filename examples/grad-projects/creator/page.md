# Page settings

Example page, made up to show content-creator's output. Written in the shape `page-setup` produces; the interview itself wasn't run.

## Goal
Leads for the service. The page builds the student's graduation project and writes the report for them, then prepares them for the defence. A viewer comments «مشروع» (or PROJECT) and gets the details and the price by DM.

## Niche and audience
A done-for-you graduation project service. The project comes first; the report is part of it.
Final-year students (computer science, engineering, IT) who don't have the time or the skills to build the project and write an 80-page report themselves.

## Language
Arabic first: light colloquial Arabic on screen, the owner's dialect in the voiceover, Western digits. English reels for a wider reach.

## Platforms
Instagram first; TikTok and YouTube Shorts with their own captions.

## Content pillars
1. **The offer:** what we take off the student's plate (lead reel: [service-ar](reels/2026-10-24-service-ar)).
2. **Proof:** finished projects and reports (real ones, with the client's permission).
3. **Useful tips:** planning and report advice that builds trust with students who later hire the page ([project-plan](reels/2026-10-24-project-plan), [report-order](reels/2026-10-24-report-order)).

## Formats and cadence
Faceless reels built in code, voiced by the page owner. 3 reels a week, 1 carousel. Build day: Sunday.

## Posting hour
Evenings, when students are working on their projects. Only post when you can reply for 30 to 60 minutes.

## Call to action
Comment «مشروع» (accept PROJECT too). The DM sends the details and the price, and asks for the major and the deadline.

## Look
Background #0E1226, paper #FBF8F1, ink #1A1E36, highlighter #FFD43B, red #E5484D, green #22B07D.
Covers: the hook in white over the document the reel is about, with a stamp.

## Banned claims
No grade guarantees, no "you'll pass for sure", no fake reviews or results. Prices only in the DM.

## Tools
Example run: built-in renderer (Node 22, ffmpeg, Playwright). No connectors used.
